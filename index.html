02 — Process

How a campaign comes together.

STEP 01

Plan the week

21 pins, 3 a day, one board. Every look matched to the book it teaches.

STEP 02

Make the creative

Nails only, no hands. Title, image and overlay must match, or the pin confuses people.

STEP 03

Schedule slowly

Like a human — wait for uploads, pause between pins, fill the tagged topics.

STEP 04

Measure honestly

Impressions, saves, clicks. First sale still pending — stated, not hidden.
