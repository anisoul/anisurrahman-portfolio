What I Learned

  • Distribution is the bottleneck. Each Pinterest pin gets a small seed audience, and saves widen it. Good content alone does not bring traffic.
  • Schedule slowly, like a human — or the bot check triggers. Wait for pages and uploads to fully load, pause between pins.
  • Title, image and overlay must match, or the pin confuses people. A pin should need no explanation.
  • Frame illustrations as a blueprint advantage, not an apology for having no photos. The guides are illustrated workbooks — canvases, sizing profiles, ledgers and practice pages.
  • Keep checkout simple for a digital product. Shipping fields do not belong on a download — I removed 4 of them from the Gumroad checkout.
  • New Pages get filtered by group moderation. Removals were about the Page having no group history, not about the content — so clean look-only posts, no links, respect each group's rules, never repost removed content.
  • Local SEO basics: Relevance, Proximity and Prominence decide local ranking; NAP (Name, Address, Phone) must stay identical everywhere; a Google Business Profile is set up in 7 steps and verified by Google before it can rank.
